## Likely objections

- **"We already have robust identity management in place."** Acme Access specializes in right-sizing access across multi-cloud environments to prevent over-privileged roles that standard tools often miss.
- **"Our current security processes are sufficient for our scale."** As you continue to grow, Acme Access automates access reviews that typically take weeks of manual spreadsheet work.
- **"We prefer to build our own internal security tools."** Acme Access provides a specialized view across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud that is difficult to build and maintain internally.
