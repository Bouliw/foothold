## Likely objections

- **"We already have built-in security and observability tools."** Our platform complements your existing stack by providing unified visibility into who can reach what across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
- **"We prefer to build our own internal security tooling."** Acme Access automates the lifecycle of access, reducing the manual toil and spreadsheet-based reviews that often slow down engineering teams.
- **"We are focused on AI-driven security right now."** Acme Access provides the foundational identity and access management needed to ensure your AI-driven security tools have accurate, right-sized data.
